# Exploring Miaoli — Picture Book Series Development Guidelines (summary)

Source: Exploring_Miaoli_Picture_Book_Development_Guidelines_EN (Miaoli English Education Resource Center).
The guidelines are "our common foundation, not restrictions". Each story is reviewed and revised together as a team before it is finalised.

## Shared design rules (all books)

- CLIL approach: local culture and English together. Every story includes Miaoli culture, nature, people and communities, history or everyday life.
- Six units per book. Each unit is a complete, self-contained story.
- Required elements: **setting, characters, mission, problem, solution, clear ending.**
- Stories are narrative-driven, engaging, interactive and educational.
- The main character stays consistent in appearance, personality, clothing, accessories and expressions.
- Illustrations are primary, and text supports them. Illustrations help readers understand the story; they are not just decoration.
- Illustration style: cute, warm, bright and colourful, with expressive characters.

## Unit working agreements (from the Unit Development Sheet)

- 12 pages per unit: 10 story pages, then a language review page (11) and an activity page (12). Whether the 12 include these two pages is still being confirmed per unit.
- 8–10 picturable vocabulary words for Pre-A1.
- Two core sentence patterns plus one recycled pattern.
- Activities must work without stickers, so a child can complete them with a pencil.
- Illustration descriptions cover setting, characters, expressions, actions and layout, and will guide AI image generation later.
- Cultural points we are unsure about are written as questions, not guessed at in the story.
