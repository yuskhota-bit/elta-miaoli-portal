## 1 · Style comparison

Angela's edits to the Mazu story shortened it to 12 pages of 13–25 words (234 in total) and moved explanation into the illustrations and notes. The Sky Mending draft ran 18–36 words a page (310 in total). Revised: 10 pages of 14–28 words (205 in total).

| What Angela's edit does (Mazu) | Sky Mending draft before | Sky Mending revised |
|---|---|---|
| Short pages: one idea and about 13–25 words a page. | Pages of 18–36 words; spreads 2, 3, 9 and 10 carry two or three ideas each. | Every page has one idea. Old pages 4+5 and 11+12 merged; the page 2 question moved to page 3. |
| One short sentence per line, easy to read aloud. | Running paragraphs. | Each sentence on its own line. |
| Fewer repeated questions and place names. | Three different questions ("Are you farming / sewing / working?"). | One repeated frame, "Do you ___ today?", kept three times so children can join in. No place names added. |
| Join-in sounds and actions: "Step, step, step.", "Shh". | No sounds in the original. | "No, no, no!" and "Clap, clap, clap!" (the extra sounds from my earlier version are removed). |
| Hard words are not core vocabulary (palanquin, sacred fire); a teacher supports them. | goddess, colorful, needle, thread. | goddess, colorful and thread cut. Nüwa, stones and needle are taught with picture support. |
| Pictures carry the meaning: speaker shown clearly, everyday clothing, ordinary lighting, no unexplained extras (the crown). | Few picture directions. | Notes ask for everyday clothing, a clearly shown girl, no incense or fire, an ordinary daytime sky. |
| Cultural points flagged for review before finalizing. | None flagged. | Nüwa's form and the cake offering flagged on pages 3 and 8. |

## 2 · Unit information

| Unit Topic: | Sky Mending Day |
|---|---|
| Story Outline | Miaoli Meow hears singing on a gray morning and learns it is Sky Mending Day. He wants to work hard to help Nüwa, but everybody rests. He learns to say thank you with cakes and songs, and the sky turns blue. |
| Learning goals: Vocabulary Words | sky, stone, rest, sing, cake, sweet, family, Grandpa, Auntie, thank you |
| Learning goals: Sentence Pattern | Do you ___ today? No, no, no! Today we rest. |
| Learning goals: Cultural Understanding | To understand that Hakka families rest on Sky Mending Day and say thank you to Nüwa with cakes and songs. |
